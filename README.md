## Overview

In ***Other Means***, you lead a nation through complex geopolitical crises. Every war has a political objective, and every decision carries cascading consequences across the military, diplomatic, economic, and domestic spheres. Victory is not always measured by territory gained, but by achieving your political goals while maintaining stability at home.

**Genre**: Grand Strategy / Geopolitical Military Simulation 
**Perspective**: Real-time with pause + Strategic Map 
**Setting**: Modern / Near-future global environment

## Key Features

- **Clausewitzian Strategy** War as an instrument of politics. Objectives are political, not purely military. 
- **Dynamic Global Map** Real-world geography with accurate terrain, chokepoints, and resource regions. 
- **Interconnected Systems** Military campaigns affect politics, economy, public opinion, and alliances. 
- **Command & Control Realism** Friction, fog of war, morale, logistics, and command chains matter. 
- **Diplomatic Depth** Alliances, proxy wars, sanctions, information warfare, and back-channel negotiations. 
- **Emergent Narratives** No two campaigns play out the same way due to deep simulation layers. 

## Gameplay

You manage four core pillars simultaneously:

1. **Military** — Force deployment, doctrine, logistics, and operational art 
2. **Politics** — Domestic support, international legitimacy, and grand strategy 
3. **Economy** — Resource management, sanctions, and war economy 
4. **Information** — Media, propaganda, cyber operations, and public perception

Success requires mastering the interplay between these domains. Purely military victories without political coherence often lead to strategic failure.
